# MeetScribe

Local meeting notes for macOS: records your meetings (Teams, Zoom, Meet…), transcribes them,
recognises who spoke, and writes a summary with decisions and to-dos — all on your Mac, with no
cloud service.

**Download:** the `.dmg` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest), then follow
[INSTALL.txt](INSTALL.txt).

Requirements: Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 13+, 8 GB memory or more, ~8 GB free disk.

The app updates itself (it reads `latest.json` here) and picks AI models that fit your Mac from
`models.json` — only models tested with MeetScribe are listed.

Always tell the other participants that you're recording.
