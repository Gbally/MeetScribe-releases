Installing MeetNouce
=====================

Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 13 or later, 8 GB memory or more,
and ~8 GB free disk space. MeetNouce picks AI models that fit your Mac (Settings → AI models).

1. Drag MeetNouce into the Applications folder.
2. Open MeetNouce from Applications.
   If macOS says it "can't be opened" / "cannot verify the developer":
     System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down → "Open Anyway" next to MeetNouce.
   (Only needed once.)
3. A welcome window guides you through the rest: your name and languages, the permissions
   (Microphone, Screen & System Audio Recording, and optionally Calendar and Notifications),
   and the AI models download (4–6 GB depending on your Mac, 10–20 minutes, once).
   After allowing Screen & System Audio Recording, restart MeetNouce when it offers to.
   For Outlook meeting names, your work calendar must be in the macOS Calendar app
   (System Settings → Internet Accounts → Microsoft Exchange).

Using it: click the icon → Start recording. Click again → Stop & create notes.
Notes, to-dos and transcripts appear in the same window a few minutes later, and are saved in
Documents/MeetingNotes.

Everything runs on your Mac: no account, no tracking, nothing about your meetings is sent anywhere.
Always tell participants that you are recording.

MeetNouce opens at login (turn it off in Settings → General).
Updates: MeetNouce checks once a day and offers new versions in its window (Update).
Uninstall: Settings → Privacy and storage → Remove MeetNouce. It deletes the engine and AI
models (several GB outside the app) too — dragging the app to the Trash would leave them behind.
