Installing MeetScribe
=====================

Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 13 or later, 16 GB RAM
recommended, and ~8 GB free disk space.

1. Drag MeetScribe into the Applications folder.
2. Open MeetScribe from Applications.
   If macOS says it "can't be opened" / "cannot verify the developer":
     System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down → "Open Anyway" next to MeetScribe.
   (Only needed once.)
3. A waveform icon appears in the menu bar (top right). Click it → "Download & set up"
   (about 6 GB, 10–20 minutes, once).
4. Allow the two permissions when asked:
     • Microphone — your voice
     • Screen & System Audio Recording — the other participants' voices
   Then quit and reopen MeetScribe (menu ⋯ → Quit).
5. Optional: allow Calendar access so meetings are named from Outlook. Your work calendar must be
   in the macOS Calendar app (System Settings → Internet Accounts → Microsoft Exchange).
6. Open ⋯ → Settings: set your name and the languages your meetings use.

Using it: click the icon → Start recording. Click again → Stop & create notes.
Notes, to-dos and transcripts appear in the same window a few minutes later, and are saved in
Documents/MeetingNotes.

Everything runs on your Mac. Always tell participants that you are recording.
